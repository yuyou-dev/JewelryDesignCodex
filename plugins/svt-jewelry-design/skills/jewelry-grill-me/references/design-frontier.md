# Jewelry Design Frontier

Use this staged frontier to choose the next questions. Grill Me completes all four discovery stages
before the separate confirmation round; it is not a flat checklist shown to the designer.

## Trigger gate

- **Ordinary clarification:** a workflow is known and only one or more identity-changing fields are
  missing. Ask one consolidated round through `$jewelry-studio` and execute after submission.
- **Grill Me:** the user explicitly requests it, or workflow, product identity, and output family are
  all unavailable after reading the message, attachments, and selected-asset context.
- **Direct execution:** workflow and product identity are clear; apply reversible creative defaults.

## Four discovery stages

Ask one to four unresolved fields in each submitted round. Facts already supplied move the round to
deeper decisions within the same stage instead of shortening the interview.

1. **Foundation:** `workflow_family`, `jewelry_type`, `starting_point`, and `delivery_count`.
   Preserve an explicit count; otherwise offer 1, 2, 4, 8, and a custom count.
2. **Meaning:** wearer or recipient, occasion, emotional intent, story, motif, symbolism, and any
   cultural boundary that materially changes the concept.
3. **Design language:** `design_system`, silhouette, visual weight, symmetry or movement, hero
   material, gemstone language, finish, craft, and reference role.
4. **Variation and delivery:** `output_family`, locked facts, flexible axes, desired exploration
   spread, presentation context, and any source asset still required.

## Candidate distance

For two or more outputs, write a candidate matrix before confirmation. Keep product category,
wearing logic, central story, and user-locked materials fixed. Each branch must change at least three
visible axes, not merely adjectives or camera treatment.

| Branch role | Distinguishing direction |
| --- | --- |
| Commercial core | Clear focal hierarchy, wearable proportions, restrained motif translation |
| Sculptural shift | Rebuilt silhouette, stronger volume, altered negative-space architecture |
| Craft signature | Distinct setting system, surface treatment, and material junctions |
| Narrative reframe | A different visible translation of the same story or symbol |
| Lightweight reduction | Airier mass, finer rhythm, simplified stone distribution |
| Architectural geometry | Ordered modules, sharper spatial logic, structural repetition |
| High-jewelry drama | Expanded scale rhythm, richer stone choreography, ceremonial presence |
| Rhythmic series code | Repeated signature unit, cadence, and collection-ready construction |

Use the first N roles as a starting scaffold, then adapt them to the user's chosen flexible axes.
Do not introduce a convertible or dual-use mechanism unless the designer requested one.

## Completion gate

The brief is ready to confirm only after all four discovery stages are submitted and the matching
workflow can execute without guessing product identity, destructive preservation intent, a required
source asset, output family, or delivery count. Summarize locked facts, flexible axes, candidate
branches, and reversible defaults, then ask for confirmation or correction. Confirmation ends Grill
Me and returns control to `$jewelry-studio`.
