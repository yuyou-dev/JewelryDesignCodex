# Jewelry Design Frontier

Use this compact decision tree to choose the next questions. It is not a checklist that must be
shown or completed in full.

## Trigger gate

- **Ordinary clarification:** a workflow is known and only one or more identity-changing fields are
  missing. Ask one consolidated round through `$jewelry-studio` and execute after submission.
- **Grill Me:** the user explicitly requests it, or workflow, product identity, and output family are
  all unavailable after reading the message, attachments, and selected-asset context.
- **Direct execution:** workflow and product identity are clear; apply reversible creative defaults.

## Question priority

Ask up to four unresolved fields per round in this order:

1. `workflow_family`: new design, remix, local edit, sketch design, retouch, model try-on, poster,
   catalog, display, grid, video, or text-only planning.
2. `jewelry_type`: ring, bracelet, necklace, pendant, earrings, brooch, or `other` with a custom name.
3. `starting_point`: text idea, source product, gemstone, sketch, selected result, model image, or
   another reference with a stated role.
4. `output_family`: standalone jewelry image, model image, campaign/poster, catalog/display set,
   video, or text only.
5. `design_system`: gold or gem-set when it changes the form vocabulary or process.
6. `design_motif`, `style_direction`, `hero_material`, and `reference_role`: ask only when the answer
   changes the concept family; otherwise default tastefully.

## Completion gate

The brief is ready to confirm when the matching workflow can execute without guessing product
identity, destructive preservation intent, a required source asset, or output family. Summarize the
resolved brief, label any reversible defaults, and ask for confirmation or correction. Confirmation
ends Grill Me and returns control to `$jewelry-studio`.
