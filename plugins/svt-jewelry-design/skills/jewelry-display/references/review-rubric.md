# Jewelry Display Review Rubric

Use this optional rubric only when the user explicitly asks to critique, rank, select, or revise
`$jewelry-display` images. It guides feedback and does not gate delivery or trigger regeneration.

## Scoring

Score each criterion from 0 to 5.

- 5: excellent, reference-grade for this criterion
- 4: good, production-usable with minor issues
- 3: acceptable but needs revision
- 2: weak, visible problem
- 1: severe failure
- 0: criterion absent or contradicted

There is no automatic pass threshold. Report scores as descriptive feedback, and revise only when
the user asks to continue from that feedback.

## Criteria

### 1. Product Readability

- The jewelry is the first visual signal.
- Category, silhouette, wearable structure, and scale are clear.
- Main stone, setting, shank/chain/clasp/earring attachment, and hero face are visible.

Failures: product too tiny, buried in props, cropped, blurred, or visually secondary.

### 2. Material Truth

- Metal reflections, gemstone behavior, jade/stone opacity, pearl luster, enamel, and pavé feel
  plausible.
- The material matches the brief and is not plastic, melted, over-saturated, or randomly changed.

Failures: lapis becomes transparent sapphire, platinum becomes yellow gold, jade looks like plastic,
or diamonds smear into noise.

### 3. Composition Quality

- Display mode is clear and not mixed with unrelated modes.
- Asymmetry feels balanced with a defined visual weight and counterweight.
- Crop, line, and product position feel deliberate.

Failures: centered despite asymmetric request, cluttered frame, product on edge without intent, or
random props with no role.

### 4. Negative Space Quality

- Quiet area is clean, breathable, and premium.
- Empty field supports product readability and does not contain random marks, text, or clutter.
- Product remains sharp and valuable even if small.

Failures: dirty background, busy shadows, stray objects, no breathing room, or unfinished-looking
blankness.

### 5. Support Credibility

- Flowers, silk, branches, vase lips, shells, stones, or hands support the jewelry with believable
  contact and gravity.
- Occlusion happens only at allowed support points.
- Chain continuity, ring closure, earring posts, bails, and clasps remain plausible.

Failures: impossible floating, broken chain, prop cutting through hero stone, hidden clasp logic, or
fabric covering key details.

### 6. Poster Drift Control

- Normal product display is text-free and product-forward.
- No invented brand names, prices, QR codes, watermarks, campaign headlines, or magazine layout.
- Props do not overpower the product as a story/campaign element.

Failures: poster layout, ad typography, brand block, decorative story stronger than product, collage
or PDP card layout unless explicitly requested.

## Revision Mapping

- Product readability below 3: increase product scale, sharpen focus plane, remove competing props.
- Material truth below 3: restate material facts, stone opacity, metal color, setting, and reflection
  behavior at the end of the prompt.
- Composition below 3: choose one display mode and rewrite the composition blueprint with product
  position, visual line, and counterweight.
- Negative space below 3: simplify background, specify quiet area location, reduce prop count.
- Support credibility below 3: rewrite support physics with exact contact point and gravity.
- Poster drift below 4: remove text, brand logic, layout blocks, price, QR, collage, and campaign
  language.
